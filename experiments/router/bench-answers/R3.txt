We multiply:

$$
17 \times 23
$$

$$
17 \times 23 = 17 \times (20 + 3) = (17 \times 20) + (17 \times 3)
$$

- $17 \times 20 = 340$
- $17 \times 3 = 51$

Now add them together:

$$
