--- QUERY ---
A store sells apples at 3 for $1 and oranges at 2 for $1. I buy 24 pieces of fruit total and spend exactly $9. How many apples and oranges?
--- ANSWER ---
We are given:

- Apples are sold at **3 for $1**
- Oranges are sold at **2 for $1**
- You buy **24 pieces of fruit** in total
- You spend **exactly $9**

We need to find how many apples and oranges you bought.

---

### Step 1: Define variables

Let:
- $ a $ = number of apples
- $ o $ = number of oranges

From the problem:
$$
a + o = 24 \quad \text{(1)}
$$
$$
\frac{a}{3} + \frac{o}{2} = 9 \quad \text{(2)}
$$

---

### Step 2: Solve the system of equations

From equation (1):
$$
a = 24 - o
$$

Substitute into equation (2):
$$
\frac{24 - o}{3} + \frac{o}{2} = 9
$$

Multiply through by 6 to eliminate denominators:
$$
2(24 - o) + 3o = 54
$$
$$
48 - 2o + 3o = 54
$$
$$
48 + o = 54
$$
$$
o = 6
$$

Now substitute back into equation (1):
$$
a + 6 = 24
$$
$$
a = 18
$$

---

### ✅ Final Answer:

You bought **18 apples** and **6 oranges**.

---

### Verification:

- Apples: $ \frac{18}{3} = 6 $ at $1 each → $6 \times 1 = $6
- Oranges: $ \frac{6}{2} = 3 $ at $1 each → $3 \times 1 = $3
- Total cost: $6 + 3 = $9 ✅

✅ Correct.
